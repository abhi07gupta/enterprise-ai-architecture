# Public safety and confidentiality boundary

- Every node, event, timestamp, topology, and outcome in this project is synthetic.
- Names are generic and vendor-neutral.
- The repository contains no production endpoint, credential, customer data,
  proprietary schema, employer architecture, internal metric, or remediation logic.
- Do not add real incident exports, screenshots, log fragments, or organization names.
- Treat generated hypotheses as investigation aids. A qualified human retains
  authority over diagnosis and remediation.
